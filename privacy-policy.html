FitSaathi Privacy Policy

Last updated: EFFECTIVE_DATE · Developer: DEVELOPER_NAME · Contact: charumadan36@gmail.com

FitSaathi ("the app") helps you log meals, water, workouts and weight. This policy explains what we collect, why, who handles it, and how you can delete it. The app is for people aged 18 and above.

1. What we collect

DataWhy
Email address (or your Google account email and name if you sign in with Google)To create your account and let you sign in.
Profile: name, age, gender, height, weight, target weight, goal, activity level, diet type, allergies, daily calorie goalTo calculate your calorie goal, hide foods that do not fit your diet, and warn about allergens.
Logs: meals, water, workouts, weight entriesTo show your diary, progress and trends, and to back them up so you do not lose them.
Crash reports (error details, device model, Android version, app version, anonymous account id)To find and fix bugs. No email, name or health values are attached.

Health and fitness information that you enter is used only to run the features above. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. We do not show ads and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

2. Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: your logs are stored in a local database so the app works offline. Reminder settings stay on your phone only.
  • On our servers (Supabase): your account, profile and logs are backed up to a Supabase database, protected so that each account can read only its own data. Data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

3. Who else handles your data

  • Supabase hosts the database and sign-in.
  • Google handles sign-in if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Sentry receives crash reports described above.

These providers process data only to provide their service to us. We do not share your data with anyone else, except if the law requires it.

4. Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules notifications on your phone. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone settings at any time.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account and all data in it at any time from Profile → Delete account in the app, or by following the steps on our account deletion page. Deletion is permanent. Crash reports are kept for a limited time by Sentry and do not contain your name or email.

6. Your rights

You can see and edit your profile in the app, export your logs as CSV files (Profile → Export my data), and delete your account. For any other request about your data, including correction or a complaint, email charumadan36@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

7. Children

FitSaathi is not meant for anyone under 18. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use access rules so that accounts cannot see each other's data, and encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your phone locked.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. If the change is significant we will also tell you in the app.

FitSaathi is a general fitness and nutrition tracker. It is not a medical service.
