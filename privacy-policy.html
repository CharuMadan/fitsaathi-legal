FitSaathi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026 · Developer: Charu · Contact and grievance email: charumadan36@gmail.com

FitSaathi ("the app") helps you log meals, water, workouts and weight. This policy explains in plain words what we collect, why, who handles it, and how you can see, correct or delete it. The app is for people aged 18 and above. We follow the principles of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. What we collect

DataWhy
Email address (or your Google account email and name if you sign in with Google)To create your account and let you sign in.
Profile: name, age, gender, height, weight, target weight, goal, activity level, diet type, allergies, daily calorie goalTo calculate your calorie goal, hide foods that do not fit your diet, and warn about allergens.
Logs: meals, water, workouts, weight entriesTo show your diary, progress and trends, and to back them up so you do not lose them.
Crash reports (error details, device model, Android version, app version, anonymous account id)To find and fix bugs. No email, name or health values are attached.

We collect only what the app needs to work. Health and fitness information that you enter is used only to run the features above. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. We do not show ads and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

2. Your consent

When you create an account you tick a box to say you are 18 or older and that you agree to these terms and this policy. That is your consent for the uses described here. If you sign in with Google, a notice on the screen tells you the same. You can take your consent back at any time (see section 7).

3. Where your data is stored

  • On your phone: your logs are stored in a local database so the app works offline. Reminder settings stay on your phone only.
  • On our servers (Supabase): your account, profile and logs are backed up to a Supabase database, protected so that each account can read only its own data. Data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. The servers of our service providers may be located outside India.

4. Who else handles your data

  • Supabase hosts the database and sign-in.
  • Google handles sign-in if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Sentry receives crash reports described above.

These providers process data only to provide their service to us. We do not share your data with anyone else, except if the law requires it.

5. Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules notifications on your phone. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone settings at any time.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your data while your account exists, because the app needs it to work. When you delete your account, your profile and all your logs are deleted from our database. The data on your phone is removed too. Crash reports are kept for a limited time by Sentry and do not contain your name or email.

7. Your rights and how to use them

  • See your data: your profile and logs are in the app, and you can download your logs as CSV files (Profile → Export my data).
  • Correct your data: change your profile and goals in the app (Profile → Edit profile and goals) and edit or remove any log.
  • Delete your data and withdraw consent: Profile → Delete account. This is permanent. You can also follow the steps on our account deletion page or email us.
  • Make a complaint or nominate someone: email charumadan36@gmail.com. You may nominate another person to use your rights if you die or cannot act for yourself. We reply within 30 days.

If you are not happy with our reply, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is open for complaints.

8. Grievance contact

Name: Charu (developer of FitSaathi). Email: charumadan36@gmail.com.

9. Security

We use access rules so that accounts cannot see each other's data, and encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your phone locked. If a data breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Children

FitSaathi is not meant for anyone under 18, and the app does not let a person under 18 finish setting up an account. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. If the change is significant we will also tell you in the app.

FitSaathi is a general fitness and nutrition tracker. It is not a medical service.
