Delete your FitSaathi account

This page explains how to delete your FitSaathi account and the data in it.

Option 1: Delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open FitSaathi and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm and tap Delete my account.

Your account is deleted straight away. You need an internet connection.

Option 2: Ask us to delete it

If you cannot open the app, for example because you uninstalled it, email us from the address you signed up with:

charumadan36@gmail.com

Use the subject line Delete my FitSaathi account and include the email address of your account. We will confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details (email address, or Google sign-in link)
  • Your profile: name, age, gender, height, weight, goal, diet and allergy preferences
  • Your meal logs, water logs, workout history and weight entries
  • Your reminder settings

What we keep

Nothing that identifies you is kept after deletion. Routine system backups, if any, are overwritten on their normal schedule.

Before you delete

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. In the app, open Profile and tap Export my data to save your meals, workouts and weight as CSV files first.

FitSaathi is a general fitness and nutrition tracker. It is not a medical service.
